Development Academy

Ages 7-12

Progression-based small group training built for players ready for a more demanding environment.

Who This Program Is Designed For

Players Ready for a Bigger Challenge

  • • Players serious about improving and training consistently
  • • Players moving beyond recreational level
  • • Players aiming for competitive teams or leagues
  • • Players ready for a more demanding training environment

This is not a beginner program.

How Selection Works

Trial Required Before Enrollment

All players must attend a trial session before joining.

  • • Assessment of current level
  • • Proper group placement
  • • Invitation-only enrollment after trial

A Step Above Regular Training

What Makes This Different

Technical Mastery

Ball control, passing quality, first touch, and fast decision-making under pressure.

Tactical Understanding

Positioning, awareness, and game intelligence taught through realistic scenarios.

Higher Intensity

More demanding sessions with stronger expectations around focus and consistency.

Ongoing Tracking

Regular coaching feedback and development tracking to guide progress over time.

Program Structure

Built Around Consistent Progress

  • • 2 sessions per week
  • • 90-minute sessions
  • • Small group focused training
  • • Age group: 7-12
  • • Progression-based training (not participation-based)

What Players Will Gain

Outcomes That Transfer to Matches

  • • Strong technical foundation
  • • Improved game awareness and decision-making
  • • Increased confidence on the ball
  • • Preparation for competitive teams and leagues

Program Commitment

Premium Training Standard

This is a higher-level training program with increased structure, intensity, and coaching focus.

Pricing is higher than our recreational program and reflects the level of training provided.
